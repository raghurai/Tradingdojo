          Trending on social

          Mentions over the last 24 hours, counted by ApeWisdom (mainly Reddit). This measures chatter, not direction or quality. Connect live data to see prices next to each ticker.

          Hot options contracts

          Social sites don't publish chatter by contract, so this lists the busiest contracts on the most-mentioned tickers. Most active ranks by volume today. Unusual keeps contracts trading at least as much as their open interest, with a minimum of 300 contracts. Quotes come from Cboe's public feed and run about 15 minutes behind.

          About this data

          Index levels start from reported closes: the S&P 500 on Oct 6, 2026, the Nasdaq Composite and Dow derived from their Oct 5 closes and Oct 6 percentage moves, the Russell 2000 on Oct 1, and the 10-year yield on Oct 2. Company prices (starting from real Oct 12, 2026 quotes, then moved by simulation), market caps from that same snapshot, volumes, the VIX, sector year-to-date figures other than Technology, Energy, Consumer Disc. and Utilities, and every tick are simulated. Do not trade on this page.

          The Trending view is not simulated. Social mentions come from ApeWisdom and options quotes from Cboe's public delayed feed.

          Sources: CompaniesMarketCap (stock list, caps, base prices), ApeWisdom, Cboe delayed quotes, Meta Trading Club, TS2 Tech, Tapeboard, Oct 2, Tapeboard, Oct 1.
